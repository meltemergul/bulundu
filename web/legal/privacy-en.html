Bulundu Privacy Policy (Version 1)

1. Data We Collect

  • Identity data: Display name, email address (for authentication only), profile photo (if provided).
  • Listing data: Title, description, category, photos, and listing coordinates (latitude/longitude).
  • Messaging: Direct messages between users regarding listings.

2. Location Privacy

"Found" listings are displayed on the map as an offset 250 m circle rather than an exact pin; however, exact coordinates are stored in the database (demo limitation).

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can delete their account at any time inside the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account). Deleting an account permanently removes the user's listings, photos, sent messages, and profile. Submitted moderation reports are retained for legitimate security interests.
